What is the cost of a POP false ceiling in Hyderabad?

The cost depends on the ceiling area, design complexity, levels, lighting cut-outs, material selection and site conditions. VERSACORE discusses the scope before preparing a quote. Share your location and requirements to arrange a site discussion and receive a project-specific estimate.

Which is better for a home, POP or gypsum ceiling?

POP allows on-site shaping and decorative detailing, while gypsum boards offer a board-based system with a consistent factory finish. The right choice depends on the design, site conditions, finish expectations and installation requirements. VERSACORE can discuss suitable options after understanding your room and design.

Do you do PVC wall panelling in Hyderabad?

VERSACORE undertakes PVC work of different types as part of its POP and interior services. The suitable panel, profile and installation approach depend on the surface, intended use and finish you want. Contact the team with a photo or brief description to discuss your requirement.

Do your services include wall panelling and die work?

Yes. VERSACORE plans wall panelling and die work as part of the overall interior scope across its service offerings. The exact detailing depends on the selected design and the space. Discuss the ceiling, wall and joinery requirements together so the finishes can be coordinated.
